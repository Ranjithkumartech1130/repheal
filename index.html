Our Story

Made by Rapheal's hands,
carried by his blessing.

In 1945, our beloved Rapheal opened the doors of a small hotel with one rule that never bent: no student left hungry. Every plate went out with complete love, every guest was greatly treated, family or stranger alike.

That same devotion now lives inside every jar we seal — handcrafted, slow-cured, and true to the recipes he first perfected at that counter. Eighty-one years later, one name still carries his blessing forward.
